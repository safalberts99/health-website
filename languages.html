Languages

Learning new languages one conversation at a time — messy, slow, and worth it.

Portuguese German

I started learning Brazilian Portuguese because of a genuine connection to the culture and people. German came later — partly practical, partly curiosity. Both languages humbled me fast. The biggest lesson so far: consistency beats intensity, and speaking badly is better than not speaking at all.

What Works For Me

🗣

Speaking from day one

I force myself to speak even when I sound terrible. Every awkward conversation is progress that flashcards cannot replace.

🎧

Listening to real content

Podcasts, music, YouTube — in the target language, even when I only catch 30%. My ear adjusts faster than I expected.

📅

Daily exposure, not daily study

I do not sit down for an hour every day. But I do hear, read, or speak something in the language every single day.

📝

Writing short texts

I write a few sentences about my day in the target language. It forces me to use grammar actively, not just recognise it.

📚

Anki for vocabulary

Spaced repetition works. I add words I actually encounter, not random lists. Keeps things relevant and sticky.
