Dance

Embracing rhythm, connection, and the joy of moving to music.

Cuban Salsa Bachata Kizomba

Dance was the thing I was most scared to try. I have zero natural rhythm and the idea of moving my body in front of others terrified me. But I walked into a Cuban Salsa class one day and something clicked. It is not about being good — it is about being present, feeling the music, and connecting with another person without words.

What Works For Me

🎵

Just show up to class

I do not practise at home much. But I never skip class. Consistent attendance is the fastest way to improve.

🤝

Dance with everyone

Different partners teach different things. I stopped avoiding people at my level and started learning from every dance.

🎶

Listen to the music daily

I play salsa, bachata, and kizomba throughout the day. The more familiar the music feels, the easier it is to move to it.

😶

Embrace the awkwardness

Everyone starts clumsy. I stopped caring about how I look and started focusing on how it feels. That shift changed everything.
