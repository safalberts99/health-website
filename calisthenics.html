Fitness & Strength

Getting stronger with bodyweight simple, scalable, and built to last.

Calisthenics Hand Training Padel

I started calisthenics because I wanted a form of training that doesn't need a gym and can grow with me. It began with basic push-ups and pull-ups and honestly, those are still the foundation of everything I do. The goal was never to look a certain way; it was to feel capable and in control of my own body.

What Works For Me

🧠

Technique first, volume later

I keep reps clean before adding more. If form breaks, the set is done.

📈

Small progressions

Harder variant, slower tempo, or one extra rep tiny changes each week.

🛌

Rest between sessions

At least one full rest day between strength sessions. Sleep matters more than an extra set.

🎯

Minimal exercises

Push, pull, squat, hinge, core 3 to 5 movements per session, nothing more.

📝

Logging every session

Reps, variant, and rest after every training. It keeps me honest and shows real progress.

⏱

35-45 minute sessions

I train 2-3 times a week, keeping sessions short and focused. Consistency beats marathon workouts.
