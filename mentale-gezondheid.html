Mental Health

Learning to take care of my mind the same way I take care of my body.

Sleep Mental State Habits

Mental health is the foundation that everything else sits on. If my head is not right, training feels pointless, food choices slip, and motivation disappears. I have learned that taking care of my mental state is not a luxury - it is the most practical thing I can do. Most of it comes down to sleep, routine, and being honest with myself about how I am actually doing.

What Works For Me

😴

Sleep is non-negotiable

7-8 hours minimum. I track when I go to bed and when I wake up. Everything improves when sleep is consistent.

📵

Phone out of the bedroom

No scrolling before sleep, no alarm-snoozing in the morning. This one change improved both my sleep and my mornings.

🚶

Daily walks

20-30 minutes outside, no music, no podcasts. Just walking and thinking - or not thinking. It resets my head.

📝

Journaling (loosely)

Not every day, but when things feel heavy. Writing it down takes the weight out of my head and onto paper.

🔄

Routine as an anchor

Morning and evening routines give structure. When the day has a shape, I feel more in control - even on hard days.

🧗

Breathing exercises

Box breathing or just focused slow breaths when stress spikes. It is simple and it actually works in the moment.
