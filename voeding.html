Nutrition

Not a diet plan - just how I have learned to eat in a way that actually supports everything else.

Healthy Eating Meal Prep

I spent a long time overthinking food - counting macros, trying different diets, reading contradicting advice. Eventually I landed on something simpler: eat mostly real food, get enough protein, and do not stress the rest. The biggest change came when I stopped treating nutrition as a problem to solve and started treating it as a daily habit to refine.

What Works For Me

🌈

Quality over quantity

I focus on nutrient-dense whole foods rather than calorie counting. More colours on my plate = more diverse nutrients.

🍗

Protein at every meal

Eggs, fish, legumes, or chicken - something substantial at every meal keeps my energy stable.

🥗

Mediterranean-ish pattern

Lots of vegetables, olive oil, fish, whole grains, nuts. Not a strict diet - just a direction that feels sustainable.

🧠

Food affects my mood

I notice a real link between what I eat and how I feel mentally. Heavy processed food = brain fog. Clean meals = clarity.

🍳

Simple meal prep

I batch-cook rice, roast vegetables, and prep protein on Sundays. It removes decision fatigue during the week.
