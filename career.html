Career & Skills

Building confidence, communication, and a career that actually fits who I am.

Career Public Speaking Social Skills

For a long time, I saw career growth as something that just happens if you work hard. But I have realised that the soft skills — speaking clearly, handling social situations, knowing what you want — matter just as much as technical ability. This page tracks what I am doing to grow in those areas.

What Works For Me

🎤

Practising speaking out loud

I record myself explaining ideas and listen back. Uncomfortable, but it shows me exactly where I mumble or lose structure.

🤝

Saying yes to social discomfort

Networking events, new groups, conversations with strangers. The discomfort fades faster than I expect every time.

📚

Reading about communication

Books on persuasion, negotiation, and storytelling. Knowing the theory does not make me great, but it gives me frameworks to practise.

🎯

Defining what I actually want

I spent years drifting without clear goals. Now I write down what I want from my career every quarter and check if my actions match.
