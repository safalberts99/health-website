Mobility & Movement

Training the nervous system to unlock range - not just stretching muscles.

Stretching Joint Health Recovery

I always thought flexibility was about how far you can stretch. Turns out, it is about how strong you are at your end range. Your nervous system blocks movement it cannot control - so the real work is building strength in stretched positions.

What Works For Me

💪

Active contraction at end-range

Instead of relaxing into a stretch, I contract the muscles as hard as I can at my limit. This teaches my nervous system the position is safe.

⏱

5-10 minutes per week

This is neurological work - more is not better. 1-2 short sessions a week is enough for real gains.

🔄

5 key movements only

Pike, pancake, front split, side split, pigeon. I rotate through these and do not add complexity.

🧠

Strength unlocks flexibility

Build strength at end-range first, then the nervous system allows more movement as a result.

🚫

No passive stretching

I stopped doing long passive holds. If I am not actively contracting in the stretch, I am not making progress.
