About this site

This website is a place where I keep track of things I am doing, things I want to do or things I have done in the past.

Not everything on this site is straight up advice. Some things are experimental and work for me. That does not mean it will work for others.

Each page has a subject I am focusing on, want to focus on or had been focusing on. The goal is simply improving each subject that represents a page. Improvements or changes will also be discussed on a page.
