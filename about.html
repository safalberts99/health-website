About Me

Who I am, why this site exists, and what you will find here.

The Story

I started The Growth Path because I wanted a place to document the things I am working on in my life -- not as advice for others, but as an honest record for myself. When I look back at where I started with fitness, nutrition, languages, or mental health, I want to see the real journey, not a polished version of it.

This site is a living document. Pages change as I learn new things, drop old habits, and figure out what actually works. Nothing here is final.

What This Site Is

A personal journal made public. Each page covers a subject I am actively improving. The tips are personal -- what works for me, not what you should do. The stats are honest, not performative. And the writing is first person, because this is my experience.

This is not a blog about self-improvement. It is not advice. It is not a course. It is just one person documenting what they are trying, what sticks, and what does not.
