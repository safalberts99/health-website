Home & Lifestyle

Building a living space and daily rhythm that supports everything else.

Organization Environment

I used to think my environment did not matter that much. But the state of my space directly affects my headspace. A cluttered room makes me feel scattered. An intentional space makes me feel calm and focused. This page is about the small changes I have made to my home and daily habits that quietly improved everything else.

What Works For Me

🛢

Tidy space, tidy mind

I spend 10 minutes each evening putting things back. It is not deep cleaning — just resetting. The morning feels different when I wake up to order.

🌞

Morning and evening routines

Same sequence every day. Wake up, water, walk, train. Wind down, read, no screens. Structure removes decision fatigue.

📷

Intentional purchases

I stopped buying things impulsively. If I want something, I wait a week. Most of the time, the urge passes.

🌱

Less stuff, more space

I decluttered aggressively. Fewer objects means less to clean, less to manage, and more mental clarity.
